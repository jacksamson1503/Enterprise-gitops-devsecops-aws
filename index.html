Manifesto

Material honesty and tactile longevity.

We reject the disposable tempo of fast retail. Every piece presented in this catalogue is produced from natural earthen clay, raw unbleached plant fibres, or solid unlacquered metals. As you live with these items, the brass will oxidize, the linen will soften, and the terracotta will retain the ambient warmth of your room.

All parcels are wrapped by hand in recycled brown kraft paper and tied with woven cotton twill ribbon. No bubble wrap, no non-biodegradable plastics.
